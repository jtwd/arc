# Room layouts, ages 2 to 5

The Stone Age pool is in `VERTICAL_SLICE.md` section 4. This is the other four launch ages.

Each age has a pool of ten layouts. A run draws from it: ages 2 to 4 take five rooms including the boss, age 5 takes four. That ratio is deliberate, because a pool roughly twice the draw is where Hades sits and it keeps runs varied without making any single room feel rare.

---

## 1. Rules that apply to every layout

- **Every pool has the same shape:** five combat, one elite, one altar, one shop, one fountain, one story. The re-skin cost of a new thread post-launch is therefore fixed and known.
- **One layout per age is built around that age's teaching mechanic** and is weighted to appear early in the age, so the lesson lands before it is tested. Marked ⬥ below.
- **The story layout carries that age's tonal marker** from `TONE.md` section 3. These are the rooms that do the narrative work, and they have no enemies.
- **Elite layouts are enclosed with a single entrance.** The elite is the room; there is nowhere to kite to.
- **Boss arenas are not in the pool.** They are the largest and most painterly rooms in the age and are specified in `BOSSES.md`.
- **No layout may require a specific lineage.** Every room must be clearable with Blunt, Edge, or Sling, held at any form.

## 2. Age of Rivers, Egypt

Ochre, turquoise, black silt. Enemies from `ENEMIES.md` section 2. Teaching mechanic: the Shield archetype, and flanking.

| Layout | Type | Shape | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Threshing floor | Combat | Open circle, grain piles as breakable cover | Jackals. The gentle opener |
| Flood steps | Combat | Terraces descending to water | Shieldbearers hold the upper terrace, so the player must climb into a frontal block |
| ⬥ The narrow gate | Combat | A corridor with a gate at the far end | Flanking is physically impossible here. The only answers are Shatter or getting behind by baiting a turn. Teaches the mechanic by removing the easy option |
| Brickworks | Combat | Drying racks that break into cover and rubble | Slingers on scaffolds above |
| The canal | Combat | Parallel water channels that slow movement | Temple bulls charge along the dry banks between them |
| Tomb mouth | Elite | Small chamber, one entrance, very dark | Embalmer. The linen grab is far worse with no room to dash |
| Painted chamber | Altar | Narrow, a painted wall | The Inventor's symbol is the painting |
| River trade | Shop | A moored barge | Flint |
| Sacred pool | Fountain | Small, still water | |
| The grain count | Story | A scribe's yard, tallies scratched everywhere | Alegus finds a grave with bread laid in it and thinks it is kind. He is right, and he is missing something. The player may see what he does not |

## 6. Production notes

Forty layouts across four ages, plus the Stone Age's ten and six boss arenas. That is fifty-six distinct spaces at launch.

**Build order.** Grey-box the full pool for an age before art passes any of it. A layout that does not work grey-boxed will not be saved by paint.

**Reuse.** Post-launch threads re-skin the shape of an existing pool rather than designing new geometry: the Viking North thread reuses the Faith and Steel shapes with new art and roster. That is the cost model the age count in `FAST_PATH.md` assumes.

**Cut order if the schedule slips.** Drop one combat layout per age, taking the pool to nine. Never drop the ⬥ layout, and never drop the story layout, because between them they carry the teaching and the tone.
