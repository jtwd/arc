# Alegus: character and voice bible

Companion to `GAME_DESIGN.md` section 2. Everything a writer, voice director, or animator needs to keep Alegus consistent.

---

## 1. Who he is

Alegus was the first human to carry fire from a lightning strike back to his people. He died that same night, of the cold, on the walk home. The fire made it. He did not.

He is **pre-cultural**. He predates every nation, religion, and language the player will see. This is the single most useful fact about him: every age is equally foreign to him, so he can be curious about Rome and the trenches in exactly the same way. He has no home era to be nostalgic for. His only home is the Hearth.

**Age:** reads as early twenties.
**Build:** lean, long-limbed, a runner not a brawler. Weapons look slightly too big for him in age 1 and fit by age 4.
**Marks:** a burn scar across his right palm from the first fire. Ochre handprints on his forearms, renewed at the Hearth between runs. He adds one new handprint for every age he clears for the first time, so his arms are a visible progression record.
**Clothing:** hide and cord in the hub. During a run the Loom re-forges his weapon but not his clothes, so a Stone Age man carries a rail lance through a server hall. This contrast is intentional and should never be "fixed" with era costumes.

---

## 2. Personality

Three words: **curious, warm, unimpressed by power.**

- He asks questions before he fights. He will ask a Roman legate what a "Rome" is mid-combat.
- He is delighted by small things: bread, glass, a chair. He is unmoved by monuments.
- He is not a hero and does not want to be. He wants to see what people became. Responsibility creeps up on him.
- He does not moralise about history. He notices. "They built a wall so tall you cannot see the sky, and then they built a bigger one to stop the first one from falling. I think they were afraid."
- He is bad at names and good at faces. He gives bosses nicknames before he learns their titles.
- He is never cruel and never sarcastic at someone weaker than him. His humour points up, at kings and machines, or at himself.

**What he is not:** Zagreus. Zagreus has a family and a grudge. Alegus has neither. His engine is wonder, not rebellion. If a line could be delivered with a smirk, it is probably wrong.

---

## 3. Arc

**Act 1, tourist (runs 1 to roughly 15).** He treats the run as sightseeing with obstacles. Death is inconvenient, not frightening. He collects observations and brings them back to the Firekeeper like a child bringing home stones.

**Act 2, witness (roughly runs 15 to 40, unlocking ages 5 to 8).** The ages get heavier. He sees the trenches. He starts asking the Firekeeper why the Loom needs him specifically, and does not like the answer: because he was there at the start, every age is partly his. He begins to feel the weight without yet accepting it.

**Act 3, custodian (first Terminus clear onward).** He understands that the Terminus is not an enemy of history but its endpoint, the silence after the last person. He chooses to keep running not to destroy it but to keep it distant. The epilogue is him at the Hearth, adding a handprint, and going again.

The ending is quiet. No throne, no reunion. He gets what he wanted in run one, to see everything, and finds it was not what he needed.

## 4. Voice direction

**Casting:** mid-range, light, unforced. Regional accent is fine but avoid anything that codes as a specific real-world period. No received pronunciation, no American frontier. A soft accent from anywhere works because he is from nowhere.

**Delivery notes:**

- Wonder is the default register. Even complaints are delivered with interest.
- He speaks slightly too fast when excited and slows right down when hurt.
- He talks to enemies as if they can answer. Most cannot. He does not mind.
- Combat barks are breath-first: grunts, exhales, and half-words before full lines.
- Never shouts a full sentence. If a line needs volume, it is a single word.

**Session plan:**

| Session | Content | Needed by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Combat efforts, hits, deaths, dash, pickups, room-clear reactions, age 1 and 2 enemy nicknames | Combat prototype (scratch), vertical slice (final) |
| 2 | Hub dialogue with Firekeeper, Cartographer, Archivist, Wren. Age intro lines for ages 1 to 5. Boss intros and defeats 1 to 5 | Alpha |
| 3 | Ages 6 to 9, Terminus dialogue, ending, Anachronism reactions, late-game hub | Beta |

## 5. Bark categories

Each category needs at least the listed count so repetition is rare. Sample lines show the tone; they are not final.

**Room clear (25).**
- "Right. What next."
- "That was a lot of teeth."
- "I think that one was somebody's, once."

**Taking damage (30, mostly efforts). Full lines only on heavy hits (8).**
- "Fair."
- "Noted."
- "That is going to leave a mark. On me. Specifically."

**Low health (10).**
- "Not yet."
- "Fire made it home. So can I."

**Death (20, one per boss plus generic).**
- "Oh. Again, then."
- To the Mammoth Mother: "You were only protecting something. I understand that."
- To the Landship: "You don't even have a face."

**Age transition (one set per age boundary, 3 variants each).**
- Into Rivers: "The stone is warm here. Who warmed it?"
- Into Empires: "Everything is straight. Who told them to make it straight?"
- Into Faith and Steel: "They put the sky inside a building."
- Into Sail: "The ground moves. The ground is not supposed to move."
- Into Revolution: "It's loud. It's so loud. Is it always this loud?"
- Into Wars: silence for three seconds, then, quietly: "Oh."
- Into Atoms: "The fire is in a box now. I don't think they should have put it in a box."

**Invention pickup (5 per Inventor).**
- Ember: "This one I know."
- Wheel: "You're telling me they just... rolled it?"
- Powder: "Whose idea was this. I want to talk to them."
- Atom: "I don't like how the paper's gone quiet."

**Boss intro (1 per boss, plus a repeat variant).**
- Legate, first time: "You have a lot of friends. Do they all agree with you?"
- Legate, repeat: "Still lined up. Still a bad idea."

**Enemy nicknames (first sighting only, 2 per enemy type).** He names before he knows. The Archivist later corrects him in the codex and he ignores it.
- Roman legionary: "Wall-man."
- Viking draugr: "Cold one."
- Trench sniper: "The quiet one. I don't like the quiet one."

## 6. Rules for writers

1. He never correctly explains a historical thing. He describes what he sees. The Archivist and the codex carry the accurate version.
2. He never says the name of a real historical person. Places and peoples are fine.
3. He never uses a word that did not exist in the age he is currently in, unless he is quoting someone. This is a soft rule for flavour, not a hard one for gameplay text.
4. He never jokes in the Age of Wars intro or the Terminus. Everywhere else, he can.
5. He addresses the Terminus as "you" and never with a title. It offers him titles. He declines them.
6. His swearing vocabulary is invented and mild. "Ash and ache." "Cold walk." Nothing modern.

## 7. Relationships

| Character | How Alegus sees them | How it changes |
|---|---|---|
| Firekeeper | A parent he cannot remember having | Learns the Firekeeper is the fire itself, the thing he carried, which is why it kept him |
| Cartographer | A merchant, faintly suspicious | Comes to understand the Cartographer sells routes because they cannot walk them any more |
| Wren | A show-off from a later age who beat him to the Terminus once | Rivalry becomes the only friendship between people from the same nowhere |
| Archivist | Someone who keeps correcting him | He keeps getting things wrong on purpose to give them something to do |
| Terminus | Not an enemy. A place. | He stops trying to end it and starts trying to keep it far away |
