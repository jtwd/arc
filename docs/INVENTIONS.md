# Inventions catalogue

Full launch list for the boon system in `GAME_DESIGN.md` section 5. Twelve Inventors, one Invention per slot each, plus two passives, one legendary, and the duo table.

Rarity multiplies the listed number: Common 1.0, Rare 1.5, Epic 2.0, Heroic 2.5. Percentages and durations scale; flat counts (charges, chances to trigger) scale in steps set per Invention.

Slots: **Attack, Special, Cast, Dash, Call**, plus **Passive** (no slot, up to two per Inventor held at once).

## Offer rules

- An altar offers three Inventions from one Inventor. Slots already filled by that Inventor are not offered again unless as a rarity upgrade.
- The first altar of a run always offers Ember, Wheel, Seed, or Forge. Later Inventors follow the age gating in the design doc.
- Duos appear only once both prerequisites are held, and at most one duo is offered per altar.
- Legendaries appear only when at least three Inventions from that Inventor are held, and never before age 3.
- The Cartographer sells a **Second Look** that lets the player reroll an altar once per run.
