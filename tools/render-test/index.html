What to judge, and what it costs

Everything on screen is drawn from flat painted shapes with pivots. No meshes, no rigs, no skinning. That is the entire argument.

Does it read as paint?

Pigment bands, edge darkening and the paper composite are doing that work. Turn all three off to see the flat vector version underneath.

Can you always find Alegus?

No ink, no edge darkening. A ring of bare paper is left around every figure instead, which is how a painter separates a subject from its ground. Turn the reserve off to see what it was holding up.

Is the transition worth it?

The wash is the signature moment of every run and the payoff for the weapon evolving. Run it a few times and decide whether it earns its animation budget.

What this saves

A jointed cut-out replaces a modelled, rigged and skinned character. On the launch scope that is roughly two person-years of 3D work neither of you can do.
